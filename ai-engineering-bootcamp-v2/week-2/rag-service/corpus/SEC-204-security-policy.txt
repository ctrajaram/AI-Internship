Northwind Robotics Information Security Policy
Author: IT Security Team
Document ID: SEC-204

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days.
Password reuse is blocked for the previous 10 passwords. Shared accounts
are prohibited without a documented exception approved by the CISO.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all staff. Hardware security
keys are issued to engineering and finance. SMS codes are not an accepted
second factor.

Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before leaving the office.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Security Team within
four hours of discovery.

Customer data may not be copied to personal devices or personal cloud
storage. Production database access requires a ticket and is granted for
a maximum of eight hours at a time.
